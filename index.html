Introduction

A unified framework for multimodal neurophysiological intelligence.

NeuroMM 2026 expands multimedia research beyond external perception to include internal human states captured by EEG, ECG, EMG, and wearable biosensors. By aligning physiological signals with synchronized video, audio, text, and contextual cues, NeuroMM defines a new computational paradigm that treats neurophysiological sensing as a core extension of multimedia computing.

The central challenge is learning robust representations and reasoning mechanisms that connect noisy biological signals with rich multimedia context. This cross-modal intelligence enables human-aware AI systems that can operate reliably in real-world environments and clinical settings.

NeuroMM Grand Challenge

Distinguishing epileptic discharges from artifacts using multimodal evidence.

The NeuroMM Challenge is built on a hospital-scale dataset collected in real clinical environments. The dataset integrates synchronized patient monitoring video with EEG, EMG, and ECG signals, annotated by professional clinicians. The benchmark targets a critical real-world problem: distinguishing epileptic discharges from artifacts and noise by leveraging cross-modal context.

Task

Detect epileptic discharges and suppress artifacts using multimodal reasoning.

Dataset

EEG/EMG/ECG with synchronized bedside video and clinician annotations.

NeuroMM Workshop

Multimodal neurophysiological reasoning and human-state modeling.

The NeuroMM workshop positions physiological intelligence as a new frontier of multimedia research, focusing on synergistic cross-modal integration between heterogeneous multimedia signals and neurophysiological data.

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Cross-modal representation learning for neurophysiological signals
  • Robust reasoning under noisy biosignals and weak supervision
  • Multimodal fusion of EEG/ECG/EMG with video, audio, and text
  • Human-state modeling in clinical or real-world environments
  • Explainable and trustworthy AI for physiological intelligence
  • Dataset creation and evaluation protocols for NeuroMM
  • Generative and predictive modeling for neurophysiological data
  • Applications: epilepsy, sleep, mental health, cardiac monitoring
